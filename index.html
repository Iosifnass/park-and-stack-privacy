Privacy Policy

Android parking game • Package: com.pawbytes.parkandstack

Effective date: November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the current version of Park & Stack (the “Game”) handles information. It applies to the Android app distributed through Google Play.

1. Information stored on your device

The Game saves gameplay information locally on your device, such as completed stages, game settings, in-game currency, parking business progress, unlocked expansions, employees, and parked vehicles. This information is used to restore your progress when you return to the Game.

These gameplay records are stored locally through Unity's PlayerPrefs-based save system. The Game does not intentionally transmit this gameplay progress to our servers.

2. Personal data and accounts

The current version does not require account creation or sign-in. Based on the current configuration, the Game does not intentionally collect or share personal data such as your name, email address, contacts, precise location, or advertising identifiers through its gameplay functions.

3. Advertising, analytics and diagnostics

The current version does not display advertisements or contain in-app purchases. Google AdMob has not been integrated into this version.

Unity Diagnostics data collection is disabled in the Android build settings. The current version is not configured to use Unity Analytics, Firebase Analytics, or Unity LevelPlay advertising services.

We will review and update this Privacy Policy before introducing advertising, analytics, or other features that change how user data is handled.

4. Third-party platforms

The Game is distributed through Google Play. Google may process information related to app downloads, device compatibility, purchases, or platform services under its own privacy policy. Those platform activities are separate from gameplay data collected by the Game.

For details, see Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Data retention and deletion

Local gameplay data normally remains on your device until you delete the Game's app data or uninstall the Game. Depending on your Android settings, device backups managed by your device or Google account may retain or restore some app data.

You can remove locally stored gameplay data using your device's app storage settings. The Game does not currently maintain a separate user account or cloud-save database from which we can delete gameplay data.

6. Contacting us

If you voluntarily contact us by email, we may receive the email address, message, and other information you choose to provide. We use that information only to respond to your inquiry and retain it only as long as reasonably needed to handle the request or meet applicable legal obligations.

7. Children's privacy

The Game is not specifically directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the Game. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us through a support request, please contact us so we can review and address the request.

8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Game's features or data practices change. In particular, adding advertising such as Google AdMob would require us to review and update the information in this policy and applicable Google Play disclosures. The effective date above will be revised when the policy changes.
